INTERNSHIPS

CEPA/10S // 2025

Defense Sector R&D

During this internship at the naval air base in Hyères, the centre entrusted me with the task of developing an innovative module for the French Navy. My objective was to design a testing infrastructure.

This design allowed me to develop my knowledge of 3D modelling and combine the expertise of machinists and engineers in multidisciplinary fields. This experience was decisive, consolidating my focus on defence systems engineering and military aeronautics.

CRTA Zagreb // 2026

Robotic and Drone Sectors R&D

My role was to implement a Visual-Inertial Odometry system on a drone and to perform algorithm benchmarking for fusion. A platform, comprising a Jetson mini-computer and sensors, retrieves topics via a ROS2 architecture for EKF, UKF, and VIO pose estimation algorithms.

This project allowed me to gain hands-on experience with tuning sensor fusion algorithms. Configure these algorithms is learned through empirical testing and not with courses. I was also able to familiarize myself with embedded systems for drones. You can find the project on GitHub:
https://github.com/timm-clv/SensorFusion/tree/main
